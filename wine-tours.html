Questions

Wine tour FAQs

Do you run private wine tours or group coaches?

Tours are private. There are no crowded coaches and no fixed timetable. You choose the regions and pace.

Which regions can we visit?

Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, Paarl, or a custom destination. A private Cape Town day can be arranged separately.

How do I enquire?

Use the enquiry form with Wine Tour selected, or WhatsApp us with your date, group size and any estates you have in mind. Availability is subject to confirmation. A request is not a confirmed booking.
