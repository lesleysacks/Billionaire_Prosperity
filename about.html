About Us

Your Journey. Our Passion. Unforgettable Experiences.

Who We Are

Private travel with a personal touch

We provide private transport and tours across Cape Town & the Cape Winelands — from airport transfers and business travel to relaxed days in the Winelands.

Our aim is simple: comfortable, dependable journeys arranged around your plans. Whether you're arriving after a long flight, heading to a meeting or spending the day exploring, we take care of the travel so you can focus on the rest.

[ Image placeholder ] assets/images/about/about-driver.jpg

Our Mission

At Billionaire Prosperity, our mission is to provide safe, reliable, affordable, and memorable travel experiences throughout Cape Town and the beautiful Western Cape.

We are committed to delivering exceptional Cape Town Tours, Winelands Tours, Wine Tours, Airport Travel, Cultural Tours, and Business Travel services, while showcasing the natural beauty, rich heritage, diverse cultures, and world-class hospitality of South Africa.

We put our customers first by providing professional service, comfortable travel, personalised experiences, and lasting memories.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised tourism and travel company in South Africa, known for excellent service, unforgettable experiences, reliability, and professionalism.

We aim to connect local and international travellers with the beauty, culture, history, and experiences of Cape Town and the Western Cape, while contributing to local communities and promoting responsible and sustainable tourism.
