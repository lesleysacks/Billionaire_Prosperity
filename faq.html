Questions, answered

Practical answers about private transport, Cape Winelands wine tours and how to enquire with Billionaire Prosperity.

FAQs

Before you travel

How do I enquire about private transport or a tour?

Use the enquiry form, or get in touch by phone, email or WhatsApp. Share where you would like to go, when you would like to travel, and anything else we should know — such as flight details, group size or luggage.

Does sending an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. Availability is subject to confirmation. A request is not a confirmed booking. We reply using the details you provide and confirm whether the journey can be arranged.

Which areas does Billionaire Prosperity serve?

We provide private transport and tours across Cape Town and the Cape Winelands.

What services can I request?

You can enquire about airport transfers, business transport, Cape Winelands wine tours, private tours, or another custom journey.

Which wine regions can we visit?

Private wine days can be planned around Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, Paarl, or a custom route. Tell us the regions or estates you have in mind and we will suggest a comfortable plan.

Can you arrange a private Cape Town day as well as a wine tour?

Yes. Wine tours are one of our services, and private tours can be shaped around the Cape Town places you would like to see. There is no fixed itinerary — share what you would like to do and we will help plan the day.

How do I choose a vehicle?

Let us know how many people are travelling and how much luggage you are bringing. We will confirm the most suitable vehicle from our small, well-kept fleet. Passenger capacity for the Suzuki Swift is 1–4; capacity for the Chery Tiggo 4 Pro is still to be confirmed.

Can I enquire on WhatsApp or by phone?

Yes. WhatsApp and phone enquiries are welcome on 079 023 5061. You can also email andremoses100.am@gmail.com. The contact page lists a second number, 062 133 8497.

What should I include for an airport transfer?

Share your flight details, pickup or drop-off point, travel date and the number of passengers when you enquire about an airport transfer. We will confirm the pickup that suits your arrival or departure.

How will you respond?

Send your enquiry using the website form, WhatsApp or email. We receive it and reply to confirm availability and the details. Nothing is treated as a confirmed booking until we have confirmed it with you.
